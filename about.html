My Story

I'm Joan Keim.

I help self-published, traditional, hybrid, and independent authors develop marketing strategies built around their actual books, not a generic formula.

"I don't start by asking how to promote your book. I start by asking why your book isn't reaching the people who would value it."

My work begins with research. I investigate the book, its market, its audience, its discoverability, and the opportunities around it before recommending a single strategy. Different books have different problems. I identify what's actually happening before proposing solutions.

I work across genres and publishing models. What I bring to every project is the same: genuine research, honest diagnosis, and a customized strategy.
