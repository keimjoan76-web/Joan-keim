My Approach to Services

Not Every Book Needs the Same Services.

Most book marketing services are sold as packages—the same bundle offered to every author regardless of their book, genre, audience, or current situation.

My approach is different. I research your book first. I identify what's actually holding it back. Then I recommend the services that address those specific issues—nothing more, nothing less.

"Before I recommend a single service, I need to understand your book— its market, its readers, its current positioning, and what may be preventing it from reaching the people who would value it."

Why Podcasts

Podcast Listeners Are Book Buyers

Podcast audiences are among the most engaged, educated, and book-oriented listeners in any medium. When a host recommends a book, their audience listens. But the podcast has to be the right one— matched to your book's actual audience.

  • I research podcasts whose audiences match your readers—not just popular shows
  • I develop personalized pitches relevant to each show's focus
  • I identify the shows where your expertise or story is genuinely relevant
Why Media

The Right Publication Reaches the Right Readers

Not all media coverage is equal. A feature in a niche publication whose readers are exactly your audience is worth far more than coverage in a large outlet whose readers have no connection to your book. I research relevance before reach.

  • I identify publications whose readers are aligned with your book
  • I research the journalists and editors covering your space
  • I develop pitches that are relevant—not generic press releases
