A book can be well written, professionally published, and full of value—and still struggle to reach the readers who would appreciate it. The problem isn't always the book. Sometimes it's discoverability, positioning, or a missed audience.

My Difference

Before I Market Your Book,
I Investigate It.

Most book marketing begins with promotion. Mine begins with questions.

Who is this book really for?

My Methodology

Research. Diagnose. Strategize. Reach.

01

Research

I investigate the book, its market, its audience, the competition, its positioning, discoverability factors, and existing platform presence.

02

Diagnose

I identify what may be preventing the book from reaching its potential readers. Different books have different problems.

03

Strategize

I develop a marketing strategy based on the actual research findings—not a generic checklist.

04

Reach

I identify and pursue the relevant readers, communities, media, podcasts, organizations, libraries, and conferences.
